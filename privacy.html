AI Company — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

AI Company is a privately operated desktop application used to manage the MinuteEdge YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public sign-up service.

Information accessed

When the channel operator authorizes Google access, AI Company may access YouTube channel identifiers and metadata, video information and publication status, and channel/video performance analytics. It uses OAuth credentials to request authorized actions, including uploading and scheduling videos.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the channel's content workflow, manage video publishing, assess performance, and diagnose failures. AI Company does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Application records and authorization credentials are maintained in the operator-controlled application environment. Access is restricted to the operator. This public website does not contain OAuth credentials, access tokens, or private channel records.

Sharing

AI Company communicates with Google/YouTube APIs to perform authorized operations. Other service providers may be used for content production; Google user data is not intentionally shared with them for advertising or resale.

Retention and deletion

Application records are retained as needed for channel operations and troubleshooting. The operator may remove locally held application data and revoke Google authorization at any time. Revocation is available from Google Account third-party connections.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact minuteedge.media@gmail.com.
