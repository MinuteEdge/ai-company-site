AI Company

AI Company is a privately operated desktop automation tool used to help manage the MinuteEdge YouTube channel. Its workflows support content planning, video production, uploads, scheduling, and performance reporting.

AI Company is currently intended for use by its operator, not as a public service. This website provides information about the application and its handling of Google and YouTube data.

Google and YouTube integration

With the operator's authorization, AI Company accesses YouTube channel information, uploads and schedules videos, and reads channel and video analytics to support its workflows. Authorization can be revoked in the operator's Google Account.
